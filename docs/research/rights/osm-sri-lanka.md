## Uses
| Use | Classification | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Basemap display (produced work) | Permitted with conditions | Visible "© OpenStreetMap contributors" attribution, placed as E1 describes for browsable maps |
| Redistribute OSM extracts | Permitted with conditions | ODbL; attribution; keep the licence |
| Derived databases (e.g. features joined to GN ids) | Permitted with conditions | **Share-alike: the derived database must be ODbL** (E1) |
| Commercial use | Permitted with conditions | Same conditions (E1) |
| Modification | Permitted with conditions | Adapted databases under ODbL |
| Compatibility | Produced works (map images, tiles shown to users) can sit inside CC BY pages with OSM attribution. **Derived databases cannot be relicensed as CC BY 4.0**; publish them as ODbL, separate from CC BY tables (data-model §2.4) | — |
