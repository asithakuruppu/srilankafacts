# Findings from combining the open spatial layers (2026-11-17)

Every number below was computed by `slf spatial findings` from the held originals and the landscape build (`pipeline/data/build/landscape/`, not in Git). Tags: ✅ computed by us in this run, 🟡 claimed by a source, ❓ assumption. All values are `modelled_estimate` rows, machine extracted, not human verified. Distances are not facts (AGENTS rule 13).

## Landscape of the whole island ✅
- Steep land (slope over the steep threshold in the method note): 14.23% of the area. Highest cell mean elevation in a GN division: 2,523.1 m (LK2312165).
- ESA WorldCover 2021: tree 72.12%, built-up 1.21%.
- Hansen: tree cover in 2000 3,943,138 ha; loss 2001-2024 233,807 ha (5.93%).
- GHSL built-up surface: 26,849 ha in 1975, 97,708 ha in 2020 (x3.64).

## Where the layers disagree with each other or with the census ✅
- WorldPop against the 2012 census, per GN division: median absolute difference 18.5%; 28.5% within 10%; 11.8% off by more than 50%. At DS level the median is 10.5%. These are different years and different methods; the difference is not an error rate of either.
- Urban: the official urban share is 17.53%. GHSL SMOD puts 72.2% of WorldPop population in GN divisions weighted by their area in urban cells (30.9% in urban centres). The two definitions measure different things; neither is wrong.
- Distance to a mapped school (Giga): 4.34% of the population sit in GN divisions more than 2 km from one. Mapped health facilities (Healthsites): 21.56% more than 5 km. Both depend on how complete the maps are ❓.
- Buildings (Overture): 8,886,949 footprints; 1.473 per occupied housing unit; 7.3% of GN divisions have fewer footprints than housing units, which points to mapping gaps ❓.
