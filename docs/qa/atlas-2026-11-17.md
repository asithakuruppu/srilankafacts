# QA: map-first atlas (2026-11-17)

Prepared by an agent. Release: local private preview `v2026.11.17-preview.1` (rebuilt from the
existing dataset builds; the earlier preview folders had been removed from this machine).
Method: [`docs/methods/atlas.md`](../methods/atlas.md). Tags: ✅ measured here · ❓ not checked.

## 1. Borders: gaps and overlaps ✅

Measured in EPSG:5235 on the files the website draws.

| Layer | Polygons | Valid coverage | Overlap between neighbours | Holes inside the island |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source GN, full resolution (spine) | 14,043 | yes | 0 | 83.94 km² (in the source: water not in any division) |
| Release `boundaries/admin3.geojson` (older map, DS) | 340 | **no** | **129.94 km²** | 168.26 km² (about 84 km² more than the source) |
| Release `boundaries/admin2.geojson` (older map, district) | 25 | **no** | **61.14 km²** | 83.73 km² |
| Atlas `overview.geojson` (DS, 150 m) | 339 | yes | 0 | 83.71 km² |
| Atlas `detail/*.json` (GN, 30 m) | 14,043 | see note | 0.00003 km² | 83.96 km² |

Note: rounding coordinates to 5 decimals (about 1 m) makes 10 of 14,043 GN polygons technically
invalid (a self-touch at a rounded vertex); they were repaired for this measurement and draw
normally. The 0.00003 km² overlap is that rounding.

The provisional DS `LK-ADM3-P0001` (Kalmunai North Sub) has no geometry in the spine, so it is
not on the map; it is listed in `manifest.json` under `units_without_geometry`.

Pipeline tests (`tests/test_atlas.py`, 4 tests): a shared border is counted once; mesh ranks do
not overlap and add up to every border; the bundle nests and has no gaps; adjacency is symmetric.

## 2. Bundle size ✅

44 MB uncompressed: 34.2 MB GN detail in 25 district files (largest 3.8 MB, loaded only when a
district is in view at zoom 8.6 or more), 4.2 MB cluster lists (one small file per indicator),
3.1 MB fine borders, under 1 MB each for the rest. First view loads `overview`, `mesh`,
`outlines`, `units`, `adjacency`, `hierarchy` and one indicator's level files.

## 3. Profile space in the browser ✅

- 6,780 precomputed pairs: largest difference between the browser's distance and the
  pipeline's, 0.00006 (rounding to 5 decimals).
- The browser's 10 nearest equal the pipeline's first 10 for 339 of 339 DS divisions.
- Axis shares of a pair sum to 100% (unit test; browser test allows ±3 for rounded display).

## 5. Not verified / known gaps

- ❓ No person has reviewed the design, the wording or the axis names.
- ❓ Not tested: Safari, Firefox, a real phone, a screen reader, keyboard-only use of the map
  itself (places are reachable from the panel lists; the canvas is not a tab stop).
- ❓ Performance on a slow phone or network is not measured; GN detail files are up to 3.8 MB.
- The map colours of the choropleth are not checked for colour-vision deficiency.
- `/explore`, indicator and place pages still draw the release boundaries (with the gaps in §1);
  only their strokes and theme colours were fixed.
- Small-denominator flags and smoothed rates are not in the atlas panel.
- No typology, no custom recipes, no drawn areas, no features below GN: not built.
- The CI fixture has no atlas bundle, so the atlas browser tests skip there.
