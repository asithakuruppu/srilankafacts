**Subject:** Census of Population and Housing 2024 — five small questions about the published tables

Dear Sir/Madam,

Further to my earlier letter about reuse of the Census of Population and Housing 2024 tables, we
have been comparing the published tables with each other before using them in Sri Lanka Facts,
a non-commercial site that shows census figures for every DS and GN division with their source.
The tables agree very closely. A few small points we could not resolve ourselves:

1. **Age bands in Population table A5 and the GN population workbook.** Total, male and female
   population agree everywhere. In eight DS divisions (Colombo, Kaduwela, Maharagama, Mirigama,
   Minipe, Harispattuwa, Hambantota and Mahiyanganaya) table A5 places between one and four
   persons in a neighbouring age band compared with the GN population workbook and the GN
   five-year age file, which agree with each other. For example, for Colombo DS, A5 shows
   60,723 persons under 15 and the GN files 60,726. Is one source preferred for age?

2. **Lighting column in Housing table A15.** The column A15 heads "Rural water supply project"
   has the same figures as the column "Generator" in the GN household file, and footnote to
   Final Report Table 11.13 lists "Generator" in "Other". May we read it as "Generator"?

3. **Firewood and gas shares in Final Report Table 11.12.** In 14 of the 52 district and national
   figures, the 2024 percentage differs by 0.1 point from the share computed from the household
   counts in table A13 (for example, firewood for Sri Lanka: 55.4 in Table 11.12; 3,381,781 of
   6,111,315 households, or 55.3 percent, from A13). Was Table 11.12 computed on a different
   base?

4. **"Attached house" in Housing table A9.** The English headings read "Attached House - 1st
   Floor", "2nd Floor", "3rd or 4th Floors" and so on, while the Sinhala and Tamil headings appear
   to give the number of floors. Do these categories give the number of floors in the building,
   or the floor on which the housing unit is?

5. **"Not relevant" in Housing tables A10–A12.** In every DS division, the "Not relevant" count
   for walls, roofs and floors equals the count of "Other" housing units in A9. Does "Not
   relevant" mean that materials were not recorded for those housing units?

Thank you for publishing these tables. A short answer to any of these would help us describe
the figures correctly.
