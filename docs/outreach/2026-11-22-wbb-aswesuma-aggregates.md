**Subject:** Request for aggregate Aswesuma statistics by Divisional Secretariat and Grama Niladhari division

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing on behalf of Sri Lanka Facts, an early-stage, independent and self-funded project building an open, map-based reference of Sri Lankan places and statistics, with every figure linked to its official source. The project's code is public at https://github.com/asithakuruppu/srilankafacts.

We would like to show, for each area, how many families receive Aswesuma benefits, alongside census and survey figures. We do **not** want any names or household details, and we will not use the published beneficiary lists. Instead, we ask whether the Board could share or publish:

1. **Counts by area.** The number of eligible families (and, if available, the number in each benefit category) by Divisional Secretariat division and, if possible, by Grama Niladhari division, for the latest list.
2. **Dates and definitions.** The reference date of the figures and the category definitions used.
3. **Reuse terms.** Whether these aggregate figures may be published with attribution to the Board, and the preferred attribution wording.

If small areas have very few families, we would be glad to receive them grouped, or with small counts suppressed, according to the Board's practice.

In return, we will attribute the Board wherever its figures are used, never alter them, and share any inconsistencies privately with the Board first.
