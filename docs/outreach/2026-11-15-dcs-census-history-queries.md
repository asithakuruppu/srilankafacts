**Subject:** Census of Population and Housing 2012 and earlier census years: four questions

Dear Sir/Madam,

Further to my earlier letter, Sri Lanka Facts (a non-commercial site that shows census figures
for every place with their source) is preparing comparisons between the 2012 and 2024 censuses
and a district population series from 1871. We have four questions we could not resolve from the
published material:

1. **2012 GN-level population.** We hold a copy of the DCS file "Population by GN division and sex
   2012" (300 pages), but could not find it on the DCS website. Could you tell us where DCS
   publishes it, or send the original file? Is there an official list linking 2012 GN and DS
   divisions to the 2024 divisions?

2. **District populations for 1981 and 2001.** Statistical Abstract 2024 Table 2.1 and Census 2024
   Final Report Table 3.3 give different figures for the same districts. For example, for Colombo
   in 1981 the Abstract shows 1,699.2 thousand and Table 3.3 shows 1,675,847. In Table 3.3 the
   1981 district figures add up to 14,629,224, while the Sri Lanka row shows 14,846,274. Which
   figures should be used for each district, and is there a note explaining the difference?

3. **Earlier census years on current boundaries.** Gampaha and Mullaitivu (1978) and Kilinochchi
   (1984) were created from other districts. Does DCS publish census populations before 1981 on
   current district boundaries, or a description of which districts gave up territory?

4. **Reuse.** As in my earlier letter: may these tables be reproduced with attribution, and what
   attribution wording does DCS prefer?

Thank you for your help.

Yours faithfully,
[name]
Sri Lanka Facts
