**Subject:** Request for reuse terms: CROPIX public APIs (geography and crop summary data)

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing on behalf of Sri Lanka Facts, an early-stage, independent and self-funded project building an open, map-based reference of Sri Lankan places and statistics, with every figure linked to its official source. The project's code is public at https://github.com/asithakuruppu/srilankafacts.

We welcome the CROPIX API gateway and its public endpoints. We would like to use some of them, and would be grateful for guidance on four points:

1. **Reuse terms.** May data from the public (no-credential) endpoints be reproduced and redistributed with attribution to the Department, for example as maps, downloadable tables and through a data interface? Does this include use by organisations that may charge for services built on the data?
2. **Which data.** We are interested only in the administrative and agricultural geography lists (for example agrarian development and ASC divisions, Mahaweli systems and blocks, agriculture zones and AI regions, with the Grama Niladhari divisions in each) and the district- and season-level crop extent summaries. We will not collect any data about individual farmers or seed handlers.
3. **Codes.** Are the GN and DS division codes used by CROPIX the same as those of the Department of Census and Statistics or the Survey Department? If not, is there a published correspondence?
4. **Attribution and use.** Is there a preferred attribution wording, and is there a request-rate limit we should respect?

In return, we will attribute the Department wherever its data is used, link back to CROPIX, never alter official figures, and share privately any inconsistencies we find.
