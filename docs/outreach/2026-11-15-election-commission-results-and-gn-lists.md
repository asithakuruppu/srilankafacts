# Draft: reuse terms for election results, and GN divisions per polling division (Election Commission of Sri Lanka)

Status: **draft** · Channel: letter or email to the Election Commission (the lead chooses the address from the Commission's contact page, `https://elections.gov.lk/en/contactus/contactus_election_commission_E.html`; never a guessed address) · Prompt: R04 · Language: English

**Subject:** Reuse of published election results, and the list of Grama Niladhari divisions in each polling division

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing on behalf of Sri Lanka Facts, an early-stage, independent and self-funded project building an open, map-based reference of Sri Lankan places and statistics, with every figure linked to its official source (code: https://github.com/asithakuruppu/srilankafacts).

We would like to present the Commission's published results, starting with the Presidential Election of 21 September 2024, by electoral district and polling division, with the Commission named as the source on every page and a link to its results page. We present results neutrally: counts, percentages and turnout as published, with no commentary. We would be grateful for guidance on four points:

1. **Reuse terms.** May the published results and the register of electors by polling division be reproduced, with attribution, on a website and in downloadable tables? Does the answer differ for commercial use, such as paid research services?
2. **Grama Niladhari divisions per polling division.** Does the Commission hold a list of the GN divisions that make up each of the 160 polling divisions, as used for the 2024 register? If so, could it be shared, and on what terms? We would use it only to relate results to census population figures; it would contain no information about individual voters.
3. **Boundaries.** Are polling division boundary maps or files held by the Commission (or by the Survey Department on its behalf), and may they be reused?
4. **Machine-readable results.** Are the 2024 results available as a spreadsheet or data file? Some result sheets on the website are scanned images, and reading them automatically risks errors that we would rather avoid.

In return, we will attribute the Commission wherever its figures are used, link to its pages, and report to the Commission, privately and before publication, any inconsistencies our checks find. For example, on the 2024 presidential results page, the Colombo North download is named `1_AA.pdf` rather than `1_A.pdf`, and some result sheets have no searchable text.

Thank you for your time.
