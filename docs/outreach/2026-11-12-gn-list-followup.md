**Subject:** Follow-up: official list of Grama Niladhari divisions in Kalmunai and Batticaloa District

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am following up on my letter of [date sent] about the administrative boundaries we use in
Sri Lanka Facts. To make the request easier to answer, I have narrowed it down to the divisions
we cannot identify from published sources.

The administrative boundary dataset published by UN OCHA, which names the Survey Department as
its source, has 65 Grama Niladhari (GN) divisions with no name. We have identified 12 of them by
comparing them with the Department of Census and Statistics' 2024 GN tables and the NSDI
geoportal. We cannot identify the remaining 53:

1. **Kalmunai DS division (Ampara District):** 29 GN divisions. The census lists 29 GN names
   for Kalmunai, but we cannot tell which name belongs to which boundary.
2. **Eravur Pattu DS division (Batticaloa District):** 7 GN divisions. Three GN names
   (Eravur 04, Eravur 05 and Ellainagar) are listed by the census, and the NSDI geoportal marks
   them as having a boundary dispute.
3. **17 small GN divisions,** almost all in Batticaloa District. None appears in the census
   tables or on the NSDI geoportal. Several are smaller than one hectare, so they may be
   digitising slivers rather than GN divisions.

The attached table lists each division with the code used in the OCHA dataset. For these 53
divisions, we would be grateful for any of the following:

- an official list of GN divisions with their names and GN numbers, or
- confirmation of which GN number or name each listed boundary represents, or
- confirmation that a listed boundary is not a GN division.

Until we hear from you, these divisions are shown on our site by code only, with a note that
the name is unconfirmed. We have not guessed any names.

Thank you again for your time.

Yours faithfully,
