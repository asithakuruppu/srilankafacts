# Opportunities register

A living record of ideas, so none get lost during planning. **Not a roadmap:** an idea moves into the roadmap only after an evaluation (prompt M01) and a decision-log entry.

**How to add an idea:** copy the template at the bottom, give it the next `OPP-` number, and add a row to the summary table. Keep it short. Mark facts ✅/🟡/❓.

**Horizon:** *Now* (Stage 0–1) · *Next* (Stage 2) · *Later* (Stage 3–4) · *Speculative* (needs research before it can even be scoped).
**Value:** 1–5 (public value and strategic value combined) · **Effort:** S (days) · M (weeks) · L (months) · XL (multi-month, needs people or money).

## Ideas

### OPP-01 Census Atlas 2024 (flagship)
- **What:** 2024 Census topics for every unit from country to GN division on a fast map, with place pages, downloads and a static API.
- **Problem:** GN-level census data exists as Excel tables 🟡 but is hard to see, compare, join to boundaries or cite.
- **Who benefits:** journalists, researchers, NGOs, local officials, citizens.
- **Why valuable:** visual, novel at this level of detail and quality, evergreen until the next census, supplies the denominators for everything else.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L. The joins and validation are the hard part.
- **Investigate first:** DCS reuse terms (R02/R04); census unit list vs COD-AB (G02).
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 1 (D-006).

### OPP-02 Geography spine and crosswalk register
- **What:** a register of all Sri Lankan geographies (admin, LA, electoral, health, …) with stable IDs, history, external-code crosswalks and GN composition.
- **Problem:** codes differ across DCS, NSDI, OCHA and others ✅. Unit counts disagree ✅. Non-admin geographies don't nest.
- **Who benefits:** every data user; developers; GIS teams; other open-data projects.
- **Why valuable:** the core moat, and reusable by the whole ecosystem.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L, ongoing maintenance.
- **Investigate first:** boundary sources and licences for LA, ED/PD and MOH; official GN membership lists.
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 0 (D-004).

### OPP-03 Population denominators for any geography
- **What:** census population, and later households and age groups, re-aggregated to any scheme (MOH area, LA, polling division, custom polygon) via GN weights.
- **Problem:** health, election and local-government data lack consistent denominators, so per-capita comparisons are rare or wrong.
- **Who benefits:** epidemiologists, journalists, local authorities, NGOs.
- **Why valuable:** unlocks per-capita indicators across sectors. Few others can do it without the spine.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M once the spine exists.
- **Investigate first:** availability of official GN lists per MOH and LA; weighting method for partial overlaps.
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

### OPP-04 "My place"
- **What:** enter a location (map click, GPS, later an address) → GN, DS, district, local authority, MOH area and polling division, plus who serves it (DS office, LA council, MOH office, police station), with a profile link.
- **Problem:** citizens often don't know which administrative units or offices cover them.
- **Who benefits:** citizens, journalists, delivery and service organisations.
- **Why valuable:** a daily-use utility and a natural search entry point that feeds traffic to profiles.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M (reverse geocoding plus office locations; office data is the hard part).
- **Investigate first:** sources for office locations and their licences; accuracy of reverse geocoding near boundaries.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-05 Sampling-frame builder
- **What:** pick a region and strata (urban/rural/estate, district, size) → a GN-level frame with population and households, probability-proportional-to-size selection and field maps.
- **Problem:** survey teams rebuild frames by hand from census tables.
- **Who benefits:** market research firms, NGOs, academics, government survey units.
- **Why valuable:** concrete, paid, repeatable. Uses data we already hold.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M (start as a service, productise later).
- **Investigate first:** demand (R05); whether DCS terms allow commercial use of GN tables; household counts at GN level.
- **Status:** idea (commercial).

### OPP-06 Catchment and market-sizing tool
- **What:** population, households and housing characteristics within a radius or travel time of a site; compare candidate sites.
- **Problem:** businesses estimate catchments with guesswork.
- **Who benefits:** retail, FMCG, banks, microfinance, insurers, health planners.
- **Why valuable:** a strong B2B use of the spine.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L (travel time needs a routing engine on OSM roads; start with radius plus area-weighted GNs).
- **Investigate first:** demand; OSM road completeness in rural areas; routing engine choice (OSRM/Valhalla).
- **Status:** idea (commercial).

### OPP-07 Election atlas
- **What:** results and turnout by polling division (presidential, parliamentary, provincial, local), mapped neutrally from official sources.
- **Problem:** results come as tables and PDFs; maps are partisan or absent.
- **Who benefits:** journalists, researchers, citizens.
- **Why valuable:** high traffic at election time; data partly parsed already (nuuuwan `elections_lk`) 🟡.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M.
- **Investigate first:** polling-division boundaries and licence; Election Commission terms; neutrality guidelines.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-08 Historical census and historical geography
- **What:** population by district since 1871 where published; later DS-level history with boundary vintages; historical place names.
- **Problem:** long-run change is hard to see because boundaries and names change.
- **Who benefits:** researchers, educators, journalists, heritage interests.
- **Why valuable:** distinctive and evergreen; matches the brainstorm's historical ambition.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L for district series; XL for reconstructed historical boundaries.
- **Investigate first:** what nuuuwan's 1971/2001/2012 parses cover ✅ (repositories exist); the census final report's historical tables (Table 3.1 covers 1871–2024 🟡); availability of historical boundary maps (Survey Department, National Archives).
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-09 MCP server and AI-ready data
- **What:** an MCP server (`search_places`, `get_place_profile`, `get_indicator`, `get_sources`) returning values with citations, plus `llms.txt`.
- **Problem:** AI assistants give unsourced or wrong numbers about Sri Lanka.
- **Who benefits:** anyone using AI assistants; developers.
- **Why valuable:** cheap to build on the static API; positions SLF as the cited source; follows the direction Data Commons has taken 🟡.
- **Difficulty / cost:** S–M.
- **Investigate first:** hosting the MCP server (serverless); evaluation set (OPP-10).
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

### OPP-10 Public evaluation set for AI answers
- **What:** 100–300 questions with exact answers and sources, drawn from SLF data, published openly; results for our MCP and popular assistants.
- **Problem:** nobody measures AI accuracy on Sri Lankan facts (the LankaData audit found no evaluation ✅).
- **Who benefits:** the AI and research community, media.
- **Why valuable:** a credibility signal, a test harness and a news hook.
- **Difficulty / cost:** S.
- **Investigate first:** licence; question design avoiding sensitive framing.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-11 Embeddable maps and charts
- **What:** `<iframe>` or script embeds for any place, indicator or map view, with attribution and source link.
- **Problem:** Sri Lankan newsrooms have little graphics capacity ❓.
- **Who benefits:** media, NGOs, bloggers.
- **Why valuable:** distribution and citations; a potential paid white-label later.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M.
- **Investigate first:** what newsrooms' CMSs allow; performance of embedded WebGL; a static-image fallback.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-12 Ecosystem coalition and shared geography IDs
- **What:** an informal working group with nuuuwan, LDF, LDL and academics to agree on geography IDs (p-codes plus crosswalks) and divide work.
- **Problem:** duplicated scraping, inconsistent IDs, bus factor of one everywhere.
- **Who benefits:** the whole open-data ecosystem.
- **Why valuable:** high leverage for little cost; reputation.
- **Difficulty / cost:** S (time and goodwill).
- **Investigate first:** interest from each party; a neutral place to host the shared crosswalk.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-13 Open trilingual gazetteer
- **What:** Sinhala, Tamil and English names, variants and transliterations for every unit, openly licensed.
- **Problem:** Tamil names missing for all GN divisions in LDL ✅; spelling variants break joins ✅.
- **Who benefits:** developers, search engines, government, OSM, Wikidata.
- **Why valuable:** a public good, and essential for Sinhala/Tamil UI and search.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M (needs human linguistic review).
- **Investigate first:** authoritative name sources (DCS reports in Sinhala/Tamil, gazettes, Survey Department).
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-14 "Sri Lanka in numbers" newsletter
- **What:** a monthly or fortnightly email with one map, one chart and one explainer.
- **Who benefits:** general audience, journalists.
- **Why valuable:** an owned audience that doesn't depend on search or social algorithms; Data For India uses a subscriber newsletter 🟡.
- **Difficulty / cost:** S per issue (needs discipline).
- **Investigate first:** PDPA-compliant list handling.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-15 Constructive errata register
- **What:** a public list of inconsistencies found in official and open data (with evidence), shared privately with the publisher first.
- **Problem:** errors such as the Ampara parent ✅ or DS code drift persist silently.
- **Who benefits:** data producers and users.
- **Why valuable:** shows rigour; can open doors with institutions if done respectfully.
- **Difficulty / cost:** S.
- **Investigate first:** tone and process; a private-first disclosure policy.
- **Status:** idea (handle carefully).

### OPP-16 Dengue and disease incidence by MOH area
- **What:** weekly or monthly dengue cases by MOH area with incidence per 100,000 using our denominators.
- **Problem:** case counts without denominators mislead; MOH-level data is scattered 🟡.
- **Who benefits:** public health, media, citizens.
- **Why valuable:** recurring, high-interest, map-native.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M after OPP-03.
- **Investigate first:** NDCU and Epidemiology Unit data access and terms; MOH boundary source; collaboration with the Dengue Atlas team 🟡.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-17 Disaster and climate-risk layer
- **What:** flood, landslide and drought history and exposure by DS/GN; DMC impact reports; river levels and rainfall (nuuuwan `lk_irrigation`, `lk_dmc` ✅).
- **Who benefits:** NGOs, insurers, planners, citizens.
- **Why valuable:** high public value; insurers may pay for exposure analysis.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L.
- **Investigate first:** DMC data terms; hazard map sources (NBRO landslide zones?) ❓.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-18 Satellite-derived indicators between censuses
- **What:** night-light change, built-up expansion, vegetation and land cover by DS/GN, updated annually; clearly labelled `modelled_estimate`.
- **Problem:** official sub-national data is updated roughly once a decade.
- **Who benefits:** researchers, development agencies, businesses.
- **Why valuable:** fresh, comparable sub-national signals.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L (processing pipelines on open data; validation needed).
- **Investigate first:** product licences (VIIRS, ESA WorldCover, GHSL); validation against census changes 2012→2024.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-19 Access to services
- **What:** distance or travel time from each GN to the nearest school, hospital, bank, post office or police station; "access deserts".
- **Who benefits:** policymakers, NGOs, businesses, journalists.
- **Why valuable:** turns facility lists into a policy-relevant measure.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L (facility data quality is the main risk).
- **Investigate first:** official facility lists and licences; OSM completeness.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-20 Data release calendar and source monitor
- **What:** watch official sites for new publications; a public calendar of expected and actual releases.
- **Who benefits:** journalists, analysts.
- **Why valuable:** useful on its own; feeds our freshness monitoring.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M (partly done by nuuuwan and LDF scrapers; coordinate).
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-21 Resolver API with SLA
- **What:** a paid, reliable API: coordinates or address → all unit IDs and profiles; bulk endpoints; uptime guarantee.
- **Who benefits:** fintech (KYC), logistics, delivery, apps.
- **Why valuable:** recurring revenue on top of the open spine.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M (operations and support are the real cost).
- **Investigate first:** demand interviews; competitors (LDL free API ✅).
- **Status:** idea (commercial).

### OPP-22 Public finance by place
- **What:** budget allocations and spending by district or local authority where data exists; decentralised budget; provincial council finance.
- **Who benefits:** citizens, journalists, councillors.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L (data is fragmented; overlaps Verité 🟡).
- **Investigate first:** a partnership with Verité; Auditor General reports; RTI.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-23 Local-authority profiles
- **What:** for each of the 341 local authorities 🟡: population, services, finances, 2025 election results, councillors (public office holders only).
- **Difficulty / cost:** L.
- **Investigate first:** LA boundaries; finance data availability.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-24 Food price atlas
- **What:** prices of key foods by market over time (CBSL and HARTI sources 🟡 to confirm), mapped.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M.
- **Investigate first:** source availability and frequency; terms.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-25 Data-literacy kits
- **What:** lesson plans and worksheets in Sinhala, Tamil and English using our maps (for example "your district in numbers").
- **Who benefits:** teachers, students.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M (needs educators).
- **Investigate first:** curriculum fit; partner schools or universities.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-26 Address normalisation and geocoding
- **What:** parse informal Sri Lankan addresses into components and locate them to GN level.
- **Problem:** addresses are inconsistent ❓; geocoding is poor; this blocks logistics, fintech and service delivery.
- **Difficulty / cost:** XL (data, ML and evaluation).
- **Investigate first:** existing efforts (postal codes in LDL ✅, Sri Lanka Post); training data; demand.
- **Status:** idea (speculative).

### OPP-27 Small-area estimates with university partners
- **What:** model-based DS/GN estimates (poverty, employment) combining surveys and census, with uncertainty.
- **Difficulty / cost:** XL (statistical expertise; access to licensed microdata).
- **Investigate first:** academic partners; DCS licensed microdata process 🟡.
- **Status:** idea (speculative).

### OPP-28 Contribute to Data Commons and Wikidata
- **What:** publish our sub-national data and IDs into Data Commons and Wikidata for wider reach.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-29 Foreign employment and remittances by district
- **What:** SLBFE departures by district, gender and skill over time, mapped (LDF has extracted some tables ✅).
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-30 "Places like this": similarity with reasons
- **What:** for any GN or DS, the most similar places nationwide by census profile, with the blocks that matched and differed. Users can change the recipe in the browser.
- **Problem:** people know their own place but have no way to find comparable places for benchmarking, programme targeting or research site selection.
- **Who benefits:** local officials, NGOs choosing comparison sites, researchers, journalists, businesses replicating a successful location.
- **Why valuable:** a genuinely new way to explore Sri Lankan data; shows the spatial-first idea in one click.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M; precomputed, static, no server ([design §6](../architecture/spatial-first-analytics.md#6-place-vectors-exploring-by-similarity)).
- **Investigate first:** which census tables are available at GN level; whether users understand similarity reasons (test in soft launch).
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 1.

### OPP-31 Draw your own area
- **What:** draw a polygon, pick a radius or select units, and get census totals and profiles for that area, with an exactness label.
- **Problem:** real questions rarely follow administrative boundaries (catchments, project areas, coastal strips).
- **Who benefits:** NGOs, planners, businesses, researchers; the free version of paid catchment work (OPP-06).
- **Why valuable:** turns geography into a query parameter.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M once GN-to-hex weights exist (G07); runs in the browser.
- **Investigate first:** building-footprint completeness and licences for weights.
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

### OPP-32 Sri Lankan place typology
- **What:** every GN assigned to one of 8–15 descriptively named types, with published method and profiles.
- **Problem:** no open small-area classification exists for Sri Lanka ❓ (to confirm in research).
- **Who benefits:** survey designers (stratification), marketers, planners, researchers.
- **Why valuable:** public good and a commercial asset for sampling frames (OPP-05).
- **Difficulty / cost:** M; the hard part is careful naming and review, not computation.
- **Investigate first:** whether DCS or universities have produced one; adviser review of names.
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

### OPP-33 Landscape vectors from satellite embeddings
- **What:** AlphaEarth annual embeddings averaged per GN/hex, used as a "landscape similarity" block and a between-census change signal.
- **Problem:** census profiles are refreshed once a decade.
- **Who benefits:** researchers, development agencies, environmental analysts.
- **Why valuable:** yearly signal at no data-collection cost.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M–L; processing at 10 m over the whole island, plus validation.
- **Investigate first:** licence (CC BY 4.0 per Google docs 🟡, conflicting secondary report), processing-platform terms, validation against 2012→2024 census change.
- **Status:** experiment for Stage 3.

### OPP-34 Change vectors 2012→2024
- **What:** how each DS division's profile moved between censuses, and which type it now resembles.
- **Problem:** change is shown indicator by indicator, so the overall shift of a place is hard to see.
- **Who benefits:** planners, researchers, journalists.
- **Why valuable:** a compact, visual story of a decade of change.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M; depends on the 2012↔2024 boundary crosswalk (G06).
- **Investigate first:** which 2012 tables match 2024 definitions.
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

### OPP-35 Two-map explorer: geography beside similarity
- **What:** the map of Sri Lanka beside a map of the same places arranged by profile; brushing one highlights the other; "near here, unlike here" and "far away, like here" in one view.
- **Problem:** the relationship between where places are and what they are like is invisible in standard portals.
- **Who benefits:** researchers, planners, journalists, curious citizens; a strong demo for funders and clients.
- **Why valuable:** the most visible expression of the vector-native core ([design §7](../architecture/vector-space-model.md#7-experience-two-maps-side-by-side)).
- **Difficulty / cost:** M; data is precomputed; the work is interaction design and accessibility.
- **Investigate first:** whether non-specialist users read the similarity map correctly (test with paper prototypes in the Stage 1 soft launch).
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

### OPP-36 "What's here": the physical fabric of every GN
- **What:** for each GN, the buildings, landmarks, land cover and distances to services inside it, rolled up into a yearly-refreshable profile block, with a zoomable map down to individual features.
- **Problem:** census profiles are refreshed once a decade and say nothing about the built and natural environment.
- **Who benefits:** planners, NGOs, researchers, businesses, residents.
- **Why valuable:** between-census signals at GN level, and the visible payoff of zooming to the physical atom ([design](../architecture/multi-scale-features.md)).
- **Difficulty / cost:** M; depends on building completeness (G08) and legal checks.
- **Investigate first:** building-source coverage and completeness; ODbL treatment of aggregates.
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

### OPP-37 Urban greenery and tree canopy by place
- **What:** canopy cover and large-tree estimates per GN and hex from the 1 m canopy height map, labelled modelled; later, derived tree crowns.
- **Problem:** no open, place-level measure of urban greenery ❓.
- **Who benefits:** municipal councils, urban planners, climate and heat researchers, journalists.
- **Why valuable:** a timely topic; strong visual stories.
- **Difficulty / cost:** M; validation against ground truth is the hard part.
- **Investigate first:** exact licence; accuracy in Sri Lanka's cloud-prone areas; imagery date (nominally 2018–2020 🟡).
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-38 Parcel geometry partnership with the Survey Department
- **What:** parcel boundaries and land use, without owner or claimant data, for surveyed areas.
- **Problem:** land questions (fragmentation, land use, exposure) can't be answered without parcels.
- **Who benefits:** researchers, planners, agriculture and disaster agencies.
- **Why valuable:** the finest land unit; strong public value if privacy is protected.
- **Difficulty / cost:** L; access, legal terms, privacy review.
- **Investigate first:** the Survey Department's data policies on its metadata portal; partial national coverage.
- **Status:** idea (partnership, Stage 4).

### OPP-39 Mapping partnership with the OSM Sri Lanka community
- **What:** feed back fixes, building tags and Sinhala/Tamil names; co-run mapping drives where completeness is weakest (identified by G08).
- **Problem:** fine-scale detail depends on open mapping; we can't map the island alone.
- **Who benefits:** everyone who uses OSM in Sri Lanka, including us.
- **Why valuable:** improves our core inputs cheaply and builds goodwill.
- **Difficulty / cost:** S; mostly coordination.
- **Investigate first:** who is active in the local OSM community; current import and mapping guidelines.
- **Status:** idea.

### OPP-40 "What do we know here?"
- **What:** click any spot and see every value whose footprint covers it, from building to district, each with its footprint drawn, its time footprint and its information floor.
- **Problem:** users can't tell how much is really known about a place, or at what resolution.
- **Who benefits:** everyone; especially researchers, planners and journalists checking what data exists for a site.
- **Why valuable:** makes data resolution visible and builds trust; follows directly from the footprint model ([design §4.1](../architecture/footprint-model.md#41-honest-what-do-we-know-here)).
- **Difficulty / cost:** M once cell covers exist.
- **Investigate first:** whether non-specialists understand stacked footprints (paper prototype).
- **Status:** proposed for Stage 2.

## Considered and parked

| Idea | Why parked |
|---|---|
| General document/PDF search | Covered by LankaData, nuuuwan `lk_datasets` and LDF Archives ✅ |
| Public AI chatbot as a headline feature | High cost and risk; MCP gives most of the value |
| Paid individual subscriptions now | Small market ❓; needs accounts and billing; services first |
| National macro dashboard as flagship | Commoditised (CBSL, Advocata, CEIC) 🟡 |
| Mirroring LankaData or LDF datasets | Licence terms forbid commercial reuse ✅ |
| Four sub-brands | No teams behind them yet |
| Real-time everything | Most sources update monthly or slower; scheduled feeds (D-020) cover the rest |
| Social media mood maps by place | No free lawful bulk access, rare geotags, unrepresentative, real misuse risk; research track only after an ethics review ([details](../architecture/live-events-and-sources.md#62-social-media-mood-park-it-for-four-reasons)) |

Details for OPP-41 to OPP-52 are in [live-events-and-sources.md](../architecture/live-events-and-sources.md) §2–§7; expand each with the template below when it is evaluated (M01).
